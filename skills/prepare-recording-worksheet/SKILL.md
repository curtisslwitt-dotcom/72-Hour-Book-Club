name: prepare-recording-worksheet
description: Select the 30 Money Dialogs and produce the expert’s asynchronous four-part recording worksheet.
---

Read the intake and question bank. For each distinct candidate score 1–5 on buyer intent, business relevance, commercial importance, evidence strength and expert advantage. Record the total out of 25, underlying decision, Q ID and rationale. Prefer independent evidence and closeness to action when scores tie. Combine equivalent decisions, retaining their Q IDs. Exclude broad FAQs with no next decision. Record exclusions and explain any lower-scoring inclusion. If fewer than 30 eligible decisions remain, return the specific gap to research before issuing a complete worksheet.

Order the selected questions by reader journey and assign D01–D30. Keep the internal ranking separate from the author worksheet. For each question write four individually tailored cues: (1) What is your direct answer to this reader? (2) What do you check, why, and when would your advice change? (3) What permitted example or evidence illustrates this particular decision? (4) What should this reader do next? Use the decision to make each cue specific. Leave the answer spaces empty.

At the top tell the expert to say the D number, speak naturally, record in any order over one or several sessions, and return audio or a transcript. They may skip a part and identify why. Deliver the worksheet without conducting an interview. Before handoff verify exactly 30 distinct decisions, four cues per decision, and a source mapping for every D ID. Save the worksheet version and mapping. Mark Awaiting expert recording and pause.

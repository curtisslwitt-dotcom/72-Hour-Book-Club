---
name: edit-expert-voice
description: Edit a completed manuscript against the expert recordings before delivering it for author review.
---

Read the expert’s original written answers or transcript samples before reading the draft. Note specific language, sentence rhythm, favored comparisons, expressions of uncertainty and how the expert gives advice. Review every chapter against its source map. Restore distinctive explanations and qualifying words. Verify direct quotes against the recording or reliable transcript. Remove invented details, inflated certainty and unsupported first-person claims. Flag unresolved substantive conflicts for the author.

Read each chapter aloud in your head. Cut throat-clearing, generic motivational endings, repeated summaries and stock transitions. Replace abstract advice with the expert’s concrete action or example. Preserve natural repetition when it carries emphasis. Example with fictional source: if the speaker says “I check the order book first,” replace “It is essential to leverage a demand-driven approach” with “I check the order book first.” Keep any conditions attached to the original statement.

Compare the edited version with the draft for dropped decisions, examples and caveats. Check all 30 rows of the writer's coverage record against identifiable passages in the edited manuscript, or the mapped subset for a sample chapter. For each, verify that the reasoning, available evidence or example, qualifications and actionable guidance survived. Naming a question or mentioning its topic is insufficient. Restore lost substance from the sources and log unresolved gaps. Compare main-text word counts before and after editing and explain substantial cuts in the private editorial record. Retain one strong explanation of shared material and point back to it where needed. Return the clean edited manuscript and a short author review sheet naming only unresolved decisions, with page or chapter and source location. Label the result Ready for author review. Author approval is a separate checkpoint.

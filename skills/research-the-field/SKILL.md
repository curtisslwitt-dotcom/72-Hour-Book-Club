---
name: research-the-field
description: Build the researched question bank for a new expert guide.
---

Collect subject, industry, specialty and expertise. Identify the reader and the decision the book should help them make. Search reader discussions, professional sources and relevant public FAQs across the decision journey. Capture source URL, date and a short evidence note. Distinguish observed wording, paraphrase and inferred questions. Cluster duplicates by the decision behind them and retain source counts. Deliver a bank targeting 180 distinct questions, with stable Q IDs, reader situation, decision, theme and evidence links. Document evidence shortages and search gaps. Claim frequency rankings only when measured. Pass the bank, source register and intake to stage 2.

name: outline-and-write
description: Resume a book project from returned expert recordings and draft the outline and full manuscript.
---

Load the frozen worksheet and returned recordings. Transcribe or use the supplied transcript. Preserve the original. Index each answer by D ID and source file plus timestamp or paragraph. Reconcile out-of-order answers and later corrections. Keep unmatched or conflicting statements in a private issue log. If audio cannot be read, request a transcript. Record missing parts without filling them in as expert testimony.

Extract the author's recurring phrases, decision rules, characteristic examples and qualifications with source locations. Outline chapters around decisions in the order a reader faces them. Give each chapter a title, purpose, mapped D IDs, opening situation, supporting answers and reader action. Account for every D ID, marking unanswered items. Then write the introduction, connected chapters and closing action plan. Use expert-supported first person, integrate sourced context with attribution and avoid invented cases, numbers or quotations. Keep a separate passage-to-source map. Send the draft, outline, transcript index, voice samples and open issues directly to the editing skill before human review.
